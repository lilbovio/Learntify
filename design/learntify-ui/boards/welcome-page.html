How Learntify works

Small ideas. Real understanding.

Every lesson follows the same steady rhythm. You can pause, revisit, or start again without losing progress.

  1. 01

    Predict

    Look at a small expression and decide what you think will happen before running it. Prediction anchors comprehension.

  2. 02

    Run

    Change the code directly in your browser, execute it instantly, and compare the real output with your prediction.

  3. 03

    Explain

    Write the mechanism in your own words. Explaining how code works is how knowledge becomes confidence in interviews.
